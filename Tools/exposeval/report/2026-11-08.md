# Skim ⇧E vs the adaCG annotator, 2026-11-08

Numbers only. Data: HDRVS adaCG subset (trainDemo8b.pkl: 1564 stills, 782 shots of two stills, one annotator), measured, never trained on. Method and caveats: `Tools/exposeval/README.md`. Errors in stops.

**The labels hold no exposure choice.** The target is a proxy: the annotator's middle, halfway between where they put "dark" and "light" (P3 γ2.4 levels), in linear light; a shot's target is the stops that bring that middle to its shoot's median (⇧E's frame) or to 18 % grey (absolute). ⇧E is measured on the label thumbnails (102 × 54, what the tool stored with each label).

## Summary

- ⇧E as shipped is **0.59 stops** (median) from the annotator, p90 1.53, r 0.54, right direction on 74% of shots that move ⅓ stop or more. Doing nothing is 0.71 / 1.80.
- The annotator's middle moves **0.37 stops per stop** of the image's median (r 0.62): they keep dark shots darker and bright shots brighter than a full match. Unclamped, ⇧E overshoots (0.77 / 2.32). The best scale on the full match is k = 0.32, and ⇧E's ±1 clamp already does about as well as that shrinkage (k × match: 0.58 / 1.55).
- The rest is scene intent, which a brightness statistic does not see. The largest misses by tag: indoor -0.50 (target -0.30, ⇧E +0.20); sunset +0.40 (target +0.49, ⇧E +0.09).

## Noise floor and checks

- Two stills of one video: the annotator kept the same pivots on 60% of shots; where they changed them the middle moved 0.41 stops (median). The image median moves 0.16 (p90 1.58) between the two stills.
- Thumbnails vs the 12 full TIFFs on hand (drawn at 320 × 180): thumbnail levels ≈ 0.905 · TIFF^1.069, so ⇧E's statistic reads -0.51 stops (max |0.65|) on thumbnails; corrected, +0.01 (max |0.15|). In ⇧E's frame a near-constant factor cancels.
- The label histograms (linear Y, 256 bins) match the full TIFF's linear Y on some stills (r 0.99 on choice86400) and its encoded Y on others (choice86500), so they are a diagnostic row only.
- Pivots at the tool's defaults on 0.2% of stills; falloffs at the defaults on all.
