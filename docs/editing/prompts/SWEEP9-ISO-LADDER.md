# Sweep 9 · ISO ladder (owner shoots it)

For prompt 09 (noise reduction): one static scene shot at every ISO, so noise is the only thing that changes.

**Gear.** The α7 III with the 35 mm F2.8. Tripod. 2 s timer or remote. RAW (the usual ARW setting).

**Scene** (indoors, one steady light, no window light, which drifts). In one frame:
- a **flat patch**: grey card or a matte wall, at least 1/10 of the frame (noise σ);
- a **black/white edge** tilted about 5°: a printed square or a door frame (detail kept, MTF);
- **fine texture**: printed text, fabric, a plant;
- **colour patches**: a ColorChecker, or saturated objects (red, green, blue, skin) (colour blotches);
- one **dark corner** that sits in the shadows.

**Camera.** Manual. f/8, focus once and switch to MF, white balance fixed (Daylight or a K value),
DRO / HDR off, Long Exposure NR off, image stabilisation off on the tripod.

**Shots.** Two rows, 18 frames.

| Row | ISO | Shutter |
|---|---|---|
| A, exposed right | 100, 200, 400, 800, 1600, 3200, 6400, 12800, 25600 | metered at ISO 100, then halved for each ISO step (same brightness) |
| B, 2 stops under | the same nine ISOs | row A's shutter ÷ 4 (dark files: where NR has the most to do) |

Don't touch the scene or the tripod between frames.

**After.** Copy the 18 ARWs into `~/LuminaEvidence/parity/sweeps/photos/sweep09/`. In Lightroom, add
them to the album "Lumina sweep 9" and run sweep 9 as `SWEEPS-OWNER.md` says (full-size exports).
