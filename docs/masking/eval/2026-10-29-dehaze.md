# Dehaze stage: built, fitted on the owner's own Death Valley edits, held-out gate held

2026-10-29. Branch `claude/dehaze-stage`. Follows `2026-10-28-sky-exposure.md`, whose finding was
that the owner's skies come from global Highlights −68 plus Dehaze, not from a sky local.

## What was built

A global `dehaze` stage after `contrast` (`dhz`, −100 … +100, also inside a `mask:` token): the
atmospheric model J = (I − A) / t + A per channel in linear light. A, the airlight, is a grey:
`airlightScale` × the photo's tone-anchor `high` (the 95th percentile of luma, pushed through
exposure). The haze weight is the blurred dark channel against A; t = 1 − ω·w, ω = dehaze ×
`omegaPerUnit`, floored at `minTransmission`. Below 0, t > 1 and the picture is pulled toward A.
A grey stays grey, A is a fixed point, a grey ramp stays monotonic (hazePower ≥ 1, ω < 1).
One maths, three copies (`LookMath`, `lookDehaze` in `LookKernels.moreSource`, `lookmath.py`);
`make parity-check` worst 0.0038. `dhz` is written only off reset, so every older look renders bit
for bit as it did; a rules file without the stage still loads. No UI: no slider, no page change.

## What it was fitted on, and why that is not a sweep

No Lightroom Dehaze sweep exists (Classic's plug-in never had the slider; `lr_cc_sweep.py presets
--sweep 4` now writes one for Lightroom CC, unexported). The Lightroom CC library on this Mac was
read offline instead (`~/LuminaEvidence/lr-catalog/index.json`): 1854 edits, 717 with their
develop XMP local, 596 of those with the RAW on the T7 and a Lightroom-rendered preview; 45 use
Dehaze, all Death Valley, Dehaze +6 … +42 (one −28), always with Highlights −55 … −86. The 43
of them already exported at full size (`~/LuminaEvidence/parity-dv-edits`, 62 edits of 50 RAWs)
are the fitting set, by the owner's choice on 2026-10-29 ("fit on the 45"), which reverses the
2026-09-30 rule that own edits are measured, never fitted, for this one stage.

`Tools/parity/fit_edits.py`: Lightroom's export vs Lumina's neutral render of the same RAW at
the edit's crop, the whole look run by the numpy mirror with candidate dehaze coefficients,
Nelder–Mead on five coefficients, mean ΔE2000 at 384 px. The confound: on whole edits the stage
also absorbs what Highlights, Contrast and the base get wrong on these photos. So the number that
counts is the held-out set, rendered for real.

## On video (the same stage, `Tools/skyeval/clips.py`)

One mid-clip frame per clip, S-Log3 decoded here, Rec.709 as is; sheets in
`~/LuminaEvidence/skyeval/video/{log,colour,card}/sheet.jpg` (the starting form, before the fit).
The friend's 5 S-Log3 clips keep their skies (0 % codec clip); dhz:+60 adds about +5 C* to the sky

C* 9 … 31, hue 277 … 298°: that is the target table for shot matching, not chart blue. On the
a7 III card (34 of 401 clips), 11 of the 22 with sky have 70 … 100 % of it at the codec top: a
diagnosis, not a grading problem. The sky mask still reads salt flats, concrete and a lit wall as
sky; it matters only once a local exists.
