# Lumina Editor: privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Lumina Editor does not collect, store or share any information about you.

- **No data leaves your Mac.** The app makes no network connections. It has no account, no
  analytics, no crash reporting of its own and no advertising.
- **Your photos.** Lumina reads only the folders and cards you choose. It never changes your
  original files. When you save, it writes rating files (.xmp) next to your photos, and keeps a
  backup copy of any such file it replaces.
- **What it keeps.** Your decisions and recent shoots are kept in the app's own folder on your Mac
  (inside `~/Library/Containers`). You can remove a shoot's working files from the app; deleting
  the app's container removes all of them.
- **Crash reports.** If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings,
  Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. That is controlled by macOS, not by the app.
